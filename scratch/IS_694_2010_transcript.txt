FOREWORD
This Indian Standard (Fourth Revision) was adopted by the Bureau of Indian Standards, after the draft finalized
by the Power Cables Sectional Committee had been approved by the Electrotechnical Division Council.
This standard was originally published in 1955 and was subsequently revised in 1964, 1977 and 1990 (per Amd 1).
Major changes in this revision include: modification of the rated voltage up to and including 450/750 V ac in line
with IEC; inclusion of heat resistant PVC insulated and sheathed cable up to 85°C and inclusion of Type D insulation
and ST3 type sheath for flexible cords for 70°C general application; inclusion of flame retardant (FR) and flame
retardant low smoke and halogen (FR-LSH) PVC cable; introduction of reference number for each type of cable;
thermal stability test included for both insulation and sheath (per Amd 1); expansion of range of single and multi
core sheathed and unsheathed cables; division into 3 sections.
Assistance drawn from IEC 60227 series (Parts 1 to 5 with amendments), IEC 60719, IEC 60228 and BS 6500:1984.
Rounding off per IS 2 : 1960.

1 GENERAL
1.1 Scope
This standard covers general requirements of single and multicore cables/cords with rigid as well as flexible
annealed bare/tinned copper and aluminium conductor, insulated and sheathed (if any) with polyvinyl chloride
(PVC) for rated voltages up to and including 450/750 V ac, 50 Hz used in electric power and lighting including
cables for outdoor and low temperature use. These cables may be used on dc systems for rated voltages
up to and including 1 125 V to earth (per Amd 1/Amd 2; originally printed 1 500 V). This standard
also includes cables with fire performance category FR (Flame retardant) and category FR-LSH (Flame
retardant low smoke and halogen) with conductor temperature not exceeding 70°C or 85°C.
NOTES
1 The term cord is used for the flexible cables up to 5 cores covering the sizes up to 2.5 mm².
2 For cables for outdoor use, the cables shall meet requirements of additional ageing test given in Table 1.
3 The cables covered are suitable for ac single or three phase (earthed or unearthed) systems for rated voltages Uo/U up to and
including 450/750 V, 50 Hz; dc up to and including 1 125 V to earth (per Amd 1).
4 Suitable where combination of ambient temperature and temperature rise due to load results in a continuous conductor
temperature not exceeding 70°C or 85°C.
5 Not covered: telephone cables, PVC data transmission cables, instrumentation cables, PVC insulated screened communication
cables (braid/wires/tapes), flexible power cord other than PVC insulated.
6 The tests specified in Sections 1, 2 and 3 shall meet the requirements specified in IS 8130 for conductors, IS 5831 for PVC
insulation and sheath and tested as per test methods given in relevant parts of IS 10810 series.
7 Categories of cables: 01 Cables for indoor installation; 02 Cables for outdoor installations; FR Flame retardant; FR-LSH Flame
retardant low smoke and halogen.
8 This standard does not cover the requirements of flame retardance test for bunched cables.
9 Sections: Section 1 General requirements; Section 2 Non-sheathed single core cables/cords for fixed and flexible wiring (per
Amd 1); Section 3 Sheathed single and multicore cables/cords for fixed and flexible wiring.

SECTION 1 GENERAL REQUIREMENTS

4 CONDUCTOR
4.1 Material (as amended by Amd 1)
The conductor shall be composed of annealed, bare or tinned high conductivity copper wires complying with
IS 8130 (Class 1 or Class 2 or Class 5). The conductor shall be composed of aluminium wires complying with
IS 8130. A separator tape made of suitable material may be applied over conductor at the discretion of the
manufacturer. The aluminium conductors shall be of Class 1 or Class 2 as per IS 8130 for sizes up to and
including 10 mm² and stranded type as per Class 2 of IS 8130 for the sizes above 10 mm².
4.2 Electrical Resistance
The resistance of each conductor at 20°C shall be in accordance with the requirements of IS 8130 for the
given class of conductor. Compliance checked by the test given in Table 1, Sl No. (i)(a).
Conductors of cables for fixed installations shall be circular solid, circular stranded or compacted
circular/shaped stranded conductors.
In case of tinned copper conductor, the persulphate test shall be conducted as per IS 8130 and IS 10810 (Part 4).

5 INSULATION
5.1 Material — polyvinyl chloride compound of the type specified for each type of cable:
a) Type A — Cables for fixed installation; b) Type C — Heat-resistant cables (HR); c) Type D — Flexible
cords (per Amd 1). The PVC shall conform to IS 5831. For single core unsheathed cables (Section 2) with fire
performance category FR and FR-LSH, the insulation shall satisfy the relevant special FR/FR-LSH properties;
for sheathed cables (Section 3) with FR/FR-LSH the insulation need not have FR/FR-LSH properties.
5.2 Application to the Conductor — insulation shall fit closely on the conductor and be removable without
damage to insulation, conductor or tin coating. Compliance checked by visual inspection (per Amd 2).
5.3 Thickness — the mean value of thickness of insulation shall be not less than the specified value for each
type and size of cable in the relevant tables of Section 2 and Section 3. The smallest of the measured values of
thickness of insulation (ti) shall not fall below the nominal value (ti) specified in the relevant table by more
than (0.1 mm + 0.1 ti).
5.4 Mechanical Properties before and after Ageing — adequate mechanical strength and elasticity; checked
by tensile strength and elongation test per Table 1 of IS 5831 for the respective type of PVC.

6 FILLER
Fillers of unvulcanized rubber or plastics compound, natural or synthetic textiles, paper or PVC; no harmful
interaction with insulation/sheath; suitable for operating temperature; not harder than the PVC used for
insulation and sheath. Fillers shall fill the spaces between cores giving a practically circular assembly; shall
not adhere to the cores; assembly may be held by a film or tape.

7 BINDER TAPE
Plastic or proof textile material; optional, by agreement between purchaser and supplier.

8 SHEATH
8.1 Material — PVC compound of type: a) Type ST1 — cables for fixed installations; b) Type ST2 — cables
sheathed with 85°C HR PVC compound; c) Type ST3 — flexible cables and cords (per Amd 2). PVC per
IS 5831 (test requirements per Table 2 of IS 5831). For FR and FR-LSH cables the sheathing compound shall
satisfy the relevant special FR/FR-LSH properties.
8.2 Application — extruded in single layer on the core (single-core) or on the assembly of cores and fillers or
inner covering (other cables); shall not adhere to cores; separator film/tape or talcum powder permitted; in
cases per Section 3 the sheath may penetrate spaces between cores forming a filling.
8.3 Thickness — mean value not less than specified for each type and size in the tables of Section 3; thickness
of PVC sheath determined by taking average of number of measurements shall be not less than nominal value
(ts) specified in relevant tables, and smallest of the measured values shall not fall below the nominal value (ts)
by more than (0.1 mm + 0.15 ts). Compliance per dimensional requirements of Section 3, test per IS 10810 (Part 6).
8.4 Mechanical Properties Before and After Ageing — per Table 2 of IS 5831 for respective type of PVC.

9 OVERALL DIMENSIONS
The mean overall dimensions of the cables shall be within the limits specified in the tables given in Section 2
and Section 3.
9.1 Ovality — difference between maximum and minimum measured values of overall diameter of sheathed
circular cables shall not exceed 15 percent of the maximum measured value at the same cross-section.

10 TESTS
Testing conducted as given in Table 1 for each category of cable listed under scope.
10.1 High Voltage Test (Water Immersion Test) — core removed from ~3 m sample of finished multicore
cable (single core: cores selected from the coil); immersed in water bath at 60 ± 3°C with ends protruding at
least 200 mm; after 24 h, 3 kV (rms) applied between conductors and water, raised to 6 kV (rms) within 10 s,
held 5 min. If a sample fails, one more sample tested, which should pass. Cores which passed shall subsequently
withstand dc 1.2 kV in the same water bath at same temperature, conductor to negative pole, water to positive
pole, for total 240 h without breakdown.
10.2 High Voltage Test (at Room Temperature) — multicore cables and cords shall withstand without
breakdown ac 3 kV (rms) or dc 7.2 kV applied 5 min per test connection. Single core cables immersed in water
at ambient temperature 1 h before testing; voltage applied between conductor and water.
10.3 Spark Test — may be carried out as alternative to high voltage test per IS 10810 (Part 44) on single core
unsheathed cables. Test voltage by thickness of insulation: up to and including 1.0 mm — 6 kV (rms); above
1.0 up to and including 1.5 mm — 10 kV; above 1.5 up to and including 2.0 mm — 15 kV; above 2.0 up to and
including 2.5 mm — 20 kV; above 2.5 mm — 25 kV.
10.4 Flammability Test — per IS 10810 (Part 53). Period of burning after removal of flame shall not exceed
60 s and unaffected portion from the lower edge of the top clamp shall be at least 50 mm.
10.5 Oxygen Index Test — per IS 10810 (Part 58) at 27 ± 2°C; oxygen index shall not be less than 29 percent.
10.6 Test for Halogen Acid Gas Evolution — per IS 10810 (Part 59); halogen acid gas evolved shall not
exceed 20 percent by weight.
10.7 Test for Temperature Index — per IS 10810 (Part 64). Per Amd 2: the minimum measured value of
temperature index shall be 250°C at which the oxygen index is 21. (Originally printed: minimum measured
value of temperature index shall be 21 percent at a temperature of 250°C.)
10.8 Smoke Density Rating — per Amd 2: test per IS 13360 (Part 6/Sec 9):2001; maximum smoke density
rating 60 percent. (Originally "under preparation".)
10.9 Additional Ageing Test (for Cables for Outdoor Use, Category 02) — 10.9.1 sample 6 m long suspended
in heating chamber at 80 ± 2°C for 168 h; immediately after, boiling water bath 8 h, then water bath at 27 ± 2°C
for 16 h; repeated on 5 successive days; ends protrude at least 200 mm. 10.9.2 a 5 m conditioned sample tested
for high voltage per 10.2 on finished cable in water bath at 60 ± 3°C; remaining conditioned sample submitted
to cold bend or cold impact test as appropriate.
10.10 Flex Test — under consideration.
10.11 Persulphate Test — for tinned copper conductor per IS 10810 (Part 4); requirements per 6.1.1 of IS 8130.

11 IDENTIFICATION
Manufacturer identified throughout the length by name or trade-mark printed, indented or embossed, on
insulation (unsheathed) or sheath (sheathed); alternatively colour identification threads approved by BIS.
Distance between consecutive markings not more than 1 m.
11.1 Durability — printed marking durable; checked by rubbing lightly ten times with cotton wool/cloth soaked in water.
11.2 Legibility — all markings clear and legible.

12 CORE IDENTIFICATION
a) Cables for fixed wiring up to and including 4 cores per Table 2; b) flexible cables/cords up to and including
25 cores per colour scheme of Table 2; c) other colour schemes by agreement.
12.1 Colours clearly identifiable and durable (durability per 11.1, ref corrected by Amd 2). Skin-type colouring
allowed if it meets testing.
12.1.1 Yellow-green combination (earthing cores): for every 15 mm length of core, one colour covers at least
30 percent and not more than 70 percent of the surface, the other the remainder.
12.2 Core identification by numbers permitted: same colour insulation numbered sequentially (Arabic numerals,
contrasting colour), yellow-green core (if any) in outer layer; numbering starts at 1 in inner layer; numbers
repeated at regular intervals, consecutive numbers inverted; single numeral underlined by dash, two numerals
one below other with dash below; spacing between consecutive numbers not exceeding 50 mm (Fig. 1).

Constituent — Code Letter: Aluminium conductor A; PVC insulation Y; PVC sheath Y; Suitable for outdoor
use OU; PVC with FR properties FR; PVC with FR-LSH properties FR-LSH; Earth continuity conductor ECC;

unsheathed FR/FR-LSH: e.g. Y (FR), Y (FR-LSH); copper multicore insulated and sheathed: YY (FR), YY (FR-LSH).
