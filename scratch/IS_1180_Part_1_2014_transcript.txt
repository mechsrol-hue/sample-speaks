===================================================================
1 SCOPE
This standard specifies the requirements and tests including standard loss levels of mineral oil-immersed,
natural air-cooled, outdoor type, double-wound distribution transformers for use in power distribution systems
with nominal system voltages up to and including 33 kV and of following types and ratings:
 a) Three phase ratings up to and including 200 kVA both non-sealed type and sealed type.
 b) Three phase ratings higher than 200 kVA, up to and including 2 500 kVA both non-sealed type and sealed type.
 c) Single phase ratings up to and including 25 kVA sealed type.

2 REFERENCES
The standards listed in Annex A constitute provisions of this standard.

3 TERMINOLOGY
3.1 Distribution Transformer — provides the final voltage transformation in the electric power distribution system.
3.2 Non-Sealed Type Transformer — has a conservator and a breather.
3.3 Sealed Type Transformer — non-breathing, so sealed there is no significant interchange with atmosphere.

4 SERVICE CONDITIONS
The provisions of IS 2026 (Part 1) shall apply.

5 GENERAL
Technical parameters including standard loss levels of three categories of distribution transformers are given
in 6, 7 and 8. Other requirements as described in 9 to 22 are applicable for all types and ratings.

21 TESTS
21.1 General — All routine, type and special tests as described in 21.2 to 21.4 performed per relevant parts of
IS 2026. Pressure and oil leakage test per 21.5.

21.2 Routine Tests (to be conducted on all units):
 a) Measurement of winding resistance [IS 2026 (Part 1)].
 b) Measurement of voltage ratio and check of phase displacement [IS 2026 (Part 1)].
 c) Measurement of short-circuit impedance (principal tapping, when applicable) and load loss at 50 percent
    and 100 percent load [IS 2026 (Part 1)].
 d) Measurement of no-load loss and current [IS 2026 (Part 1)].
 e) Measurement of insulation resistance [IS 2026 (Part 1)].
 f) Induced over-voltage withstand test [IS 2026 (Part 3)].
 g) Separate-source voltage withstand test [IS 2026 (Part 3)].
 h) Pressure test (see 21.5).
 j) Oil leakage test (see 21.5).

21.3 Type Tests (to be conducted on one unit):
 a) Lightning impulse test [IS 2026 (Part 3)].
 b) Temperature-rise test [IS 2026 (Part 2)].
 c) Short-circuit withstand test [IS 2026 (Part 5)] (up to 200 kVA).
 d) Pressure test (see 21.5).

21.4 Special Tests (to be conducted on one unit, by mutual agreement):
 a) Determination of sound levels [IS 2026 (Part 10)].
 b) Short-circuit withstand test [IS 2026 (Part 5)] (above 200 kVA).
 c) No load current at 112.5 percent voltage.
 d) Paint adhesion tests per ASTM D3359 (Tape test).
 e) BDV and moisture content of oil in the transformer (IS 335).

21.5 Pressure and Oil Leakage Test
21.5.1 For Transformers up to 200 kVA
21.5.1.1 Pressure test (type test): air pressure of 80 kPa for 30 min and vacuum of 250 mm mercury for 30 min.
  Permanent deflection of flat plates: Up to 750 mm → 5 mm; 751 to 1 250 mm → 6.5 mm.
21.5.1.2 Pressure (routine test): a) Non-sealed plain tanks 35 kPa for 10 min; b) Corrugated tanks 15 kPa for
  10 min; c) Sealed type 80 kPa for 10 min. No leakage.
21.5.1.3 Oil leakage test (routine test): twice normal head at base for 8 h; corrugated 15 kPa at top for 6 h.

21.5.2 For Transformers Above 200 kVA and Up to Including 2 500 kVA
21.5.2.1 Pressure test (Type test): 80 kPa for 30 min and vacuum of 500 mm mercury for 30 min.
  Permanent deflection: Up to 750 mm → 5.0 mm; 751 to 1 250 mm → 6.5 mm; 1 251 to 1 750 mm → 8.0 mm;
  Above 1 751 mm → 9.0 mm.
21.5.2.2 Pressure test (routine test): a) Plain tanks 35 kPa for 10 min; b) Corrugated tanks 15 kPa for 10 min.
21.5.2.3 Oil leakage test (routine test): twice normal head at base for 8 h; corrugated 15 kPa at top for 6 h.

21.5.3 For Single Phase Distribution Transformers Up to Including 25 kVA
21.5.3.1 Pressure test (type test): air pressure of 100 kPa above atmospheric for 30 min. No leakage/deformation.
21.5.3.2 Pressure (routine test): 35 kPa above atmosphere for 10 min. No leakage.
21.5.3.3 Oil leakage test (routine test): twice normal head at base for 6 h; corrugated 15 kPa at top for 6 h.

PARAMETERIZATION DIMENSIONS FOR THIS STANDARD:
This standard varies limits by:
  category — one of: "3ph<=200kVA", "3ph>200kVA", "1ph<=25kVA"
  rating (kVA) — the standard rating
  energyLevel — one of: "Level 1", "Level 2", "Level 3" (corresponding to 3-star, 4-star, 5-star)
  voltageClass — one of: "upto 11 kV", "above 11 upto 22 kV", "above 22 upto 33 kV"

Category "3ph<=200kVA" ratings (Table 3): 16, 25, 63, 100, 160, 200
Category "3ph>200kVA" ratings (Table 6): 250, 315, 400, 500, 630, 1000, 1250, 1600, 2000, 2500
Category "1ph<=25kVA" ratings (Table 9): 5, 10, 16, 25

Voltage class loss adders:
  3ph categories: above 11 upto 22 kV = +5%; above 22 upto 33 kV = +7.5% over 11 kV base loss.
  1ph category: above 11 upto 22 kV = +7.5%; above 22 upto 33 kV = +10% over 11 kV base loss.

BIL by voltage (kVp): 3.3→40, 6.6→60, 11→75, 22→125, 33→170.
