FOREWORD
Third revision, aligned with IEC 60669-1:2017. First published 1966, revised 1986 and 1997. Significant changes:
scope changes for motor and lamp load switches; new definitions; additional marking; 13 A switches included;
flexible-conductor requirements in 12; pilot light units; new test for self-ballasted lamp loads (19.3); Table 23
redrawn as Table 9 covering normal, mini and micro-gap switches; informative Annex C (circuit development
for 19.3) and Annex D (insulation-piercing terminals). Rounding per IS 2 : 2022.

1 SCOPE
1.1 Manually operated general-purpose switches for ac only, rated voltage ≤ 440 V, rated current ≤ 63 A, for
household and similar fixed electrical installations, indoors or outdoors.
1.2 Rated current limited to 16 A for switches with screwless terminals (also for insulation piercing terminals per
Annex D).
1.3 Loads controlled in normal use: a) tungsten filament lamp circuits; b) externally ballasted lamp circuits (LED,
CFL, fluorescent); c) self-ballasted lamp circuits (LEDi, CFLi); d) substantially resistive loads with power factor
≥ 0.95; e) single-phase motor loads ≤ 3 A at 250 V (750 VA), power factor ≥ 0.8 (switches ≥ 10 A, momentary
switches ≥ 6 A, without additional tests).
1.4 Also applies to boxes for switches (except flush mounting boxes — see IS 14772). 1.5 Also applies to switches
with pilot lights, time-delay devices, combinations (except with fuses), and switches with facilities for outlet and
retention of flexible cables (Annex A). Ambient: normally ≤ 35 °C, occasionally 45 °C.
1.6 Special conditions (ships, vehicles, hazardous locations) may need special construction. 1.7 Does not apply to
circuit-breakers, appliance switches, in-line cord switches, cable-reel switches.

10 PROTECTION AGAINST ELECTRIC SHOCK
10.1 Live parts not accessible after removal of parts removable without tool; standard test finger with 40-50 V
indicator; smallest and largest conductors per 12. Thermoplastic/elastomeric enclosures: additional test at
(35 ± 2) °C, 75 N for 1 min via test probe 11 of IS 1401 (10 N on thin-walled knock-outs); no deformation making
live parts touchable. 10.2 Knobs/levers/push-buttons/rockers of insulating material unless double/reinforced
insulation or earthed. 10.3 Accessible parts of insulating material except small isolated screws, actuating members
per 10.2, metal covers per 10.3.2/10.3.3 (insulating linings/barriers; or earthing via fixing screws with low
resistance). 10.4 Metal mechanism parts shall not protrude from enclosure (key-operated: insulated from live
parts). 10.5 Mechanism metal parts inaccessible; insulated from accessible metal parts/frames/fixing screws unless
creepage/clearances ≥ 2 × Table 9 values or earthed. 10.6 Indirect operation (key/cord/chain/rod): touching parts
insulated from live parts. 10.7 Replaceable pull cord fitted without touching live parts.

24 RESISTANCE OF INSULATING MATERIAL TO ABNORMAL HEAT, FIRE, TRACKING
24.1 Glow-wire per IS 11000 (Part 2/Sec 1): 850 °C for parts retaining current-carrying/earthing parts (650 °C for
earth-terminal-in-box parts); 650 °C for other parts even if in contact. Pass: no visible flame/sustained glowing, or
extinguish within 30 s of removal; no ignition of tissue/scorching of board. Small parts (15 mm circle / 8 mm rule)
and ceramic exempt; 24 h conditioning; one specimen (doubt: two more).
24.2 Tracking (IP > IPX0, parts retaining live parts): PTI 175 V, solution A, 50 drops, (30 ± 5) s interval, no
flashover/breakdown; ceramic exempt.

25 RESISTANCE TO RUSTING
Degrease; immerse 10 min in 10 percent ammonium chloride at (27 ± 5) °C; 10 min in saturated moisture box at
(27 ± 5) °C, RH ≥ 90 percent; dry 10 min at (100 ± 5) °C: no rust (sharp-edge traces/yellowish removable film
ignored; greased springs tested only in doubt).
