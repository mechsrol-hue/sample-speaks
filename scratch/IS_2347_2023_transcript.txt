FOREWORD
This Indian Standard (Seventh Revision) was adopted by the Bureau of Indian Standards, after the draft
finalized by Utensils, Cutlery Domestic Hardware Sectional Committee had been approved by the Mechanical
Engineering Division Council. First published 1963; revised 1966, 1974, 1987, 1995, 2006 and 2017.
Major modifications in this revision include: new terminology; tolerance on gross capacity; aluminium alloy
grades per IS 21; additional requirements for electric pressure cooker; glass lid for serving; visual pressure
indicator; chain for linking PRD with vent tube; requirement for 'change in shape'; internal level marking;
pressure drop after PRD operation; cooker fastened by screw clamps / locking device under tests for removal
of lid under pressure; test for effectiveness for induction bottom.
SI units: Pressure 1 Pa (Pascal) = 1 N/m2; 1 kgf/mm2 = 9.806 650 MPa.

1 SCOPE
This standard covers the requirements for domestic pressure cookers. This standard does not cover
microwaveable pressure cookers.

2 REFERENCES — standards listed in Annex A.

3 TERMINOLOGY
3.1 Pressure Cooker — A closed domestic pressure cooking vessel for use with external/integral heat source
and capable of maintaining nominal cooking steam pressure over 0.1 kgf/cm2 and up to 1.0 kgf/cm2 gauge nominal.
3.2 Gross Nominal Capacity of the Cooker — The full water capacity of the vessel, total internal volume with lid in position.
3.3 Cooking Capacity — maximum cooking capacity of the vessel is two-third of the capacity as specified in 3.9.
3.4 Container — Vessel placed inside the pressure cooker for cooking foods separately (separator).
3.5 Grid — used inside a pressure cooker to ensure gap between separator and base.
3.6 Nominal Cooking Pressure — pressure at which the cooker operates, within a limit of ± 20 percent of the
declared nominal cooking steam pressure.
3.7 Pressure Regulating Device (PRD) — Device which regulates the pressure inside the cooker during use.
3.8 Safety Relief Device — Device which prevents the cooker from exceeding the safety pressure. Could be
destructive or resettable type. A cooker using gasket release system (GRS) shall also have additional safety relief device.
3.9 Body Capacity — Maximum quantity of water contained in the cooker body, on a flat, horizontal surface.
3.9.1 Body capacity shall be more than 2/3rd of the gross nominal capacity as given in 3.2.
3.10 Greatest Nominal Cooking Pressure — Maximum pressure achieved during test for PRD (see 8.3) in
first five readings of operations of PRD.

4 CAPACITIES
The gross capacity of pressure cookers shall be from 1 litre to 24 litre and shall be expressed only in complete
or half litre units. The tolerance on the gross capacity shall be +499/0 ml (i.e. +499 ml, −0 ml). For pressure
cooker with integral electrical heating (see 8.13), gross capacity shall be from 1 litre to 10 litre.
4.1 The gross capacity of the cooker shall be tested as given in Annex B.
4.2 Body Capacity — The body of the cooker shall be kept on flat and horizontal surface, filled with water up
to the brim; the volume of water measured shall be the body capacity.

5 MATERIALS
5.1 Body, Lid, Containers and Grid
5.1.1 Body/lid material: aluminium alloy designations 19000, 19500, 31000, 40800 of IS 21 or stainless steel
X04Cr19Ni9/X07Cr18Ni9 of IS 5522 or X8Cr16Mn8Cu2Ni2N of IS 15997.
5.1.3 Composite bottom SS cooker shall conform to IS 15960. Protector plate may be X07Cr17 (grade 430) per IS 6911.
5.1.4 Induction friendly base material as per 430 or 439 series of IS 6911.
5.1.5 3-ply body: inner X04Cr19Ni9 (304) of IS 5522, middle aluminium per IS 21, outer X07Cr17 (430)/X02Cr18Ti (439) of IS 6911.
5.2 Safety Relief Device
5.2.1 Destructive Type — fusible plug chemical composition such that it melts/ejects before a gauge pressure
greater than 3 kgf/cm2 (300 kN/m2 approximately) is reached. Maximum lead content shall not exceed 0.05 percent
by mass in fusible plug pellet.
5.2.2 Resettable Type — aluminium alloy / stainless steel / brass per referenced specs; spring SS per IS 4454 (Part 4).
5.3 Gasket — shall conform to IS 7466.
5.4 Handles and Knobs — construction and material per IS 13395.
5.5 Pressure Regulating Device — metallic part of PRD (except vent seal pin) brass per IS 319 or SS.
5.10 Tempered glass lid — conform to performance requirements 8.15. Minimum thickness of glass shall be 3.5 mm.
SS rim, if provided, material per IS 5522 or IS 15997 and rim thickness shall be 0.25 mm minimum.
5.11 Visual Pressure Indicator — indicates there is pressure inside the cooker (more than 0.04 kgf/cm2 or 4 kPa).
5.12 Gasket Release System — gasket shall comply IS 7466; GRS shall comply with H-1.2.

6 CONSTRUCTION
6.1 Pressure Regulating Device — free fall dead weight valve.
6.1.1 Minimum inside diameter of vent pipe shall be 2.5 mm.
6.1.5 The vent pipe nut shall have a minimum of two holes; combined area of holes more than area of vent pipe hole.
6.1.6 Metallic chain for linking PRD with vent tube; chain of brass or coated steel.
6.2 Safety Relief Device — independent safety relief device, destructible or resettable or both.
6.3 Temperature Responsive Safety Relief Device.
6.3.1 A temperature responsive safety relief device shall comply to Annex C.
6.3.2 If the safety pressure relief device is not of the fusible type, the cooker shall satisfy the type test prescribed in Annex D.
6.3.3 In case of electrical pressure cooker, in addition to 6.3.2, a thermal fuse shall be provided. The thermal fuse shall
be deemed to have passed the test if it functions when the steam reaches a gauge pressure between 1.25 and 3 times
the greatest nominal cooking pressure.
6.4 Handles and Knobs per IS 13395.
6.7 Internal Level Marking — may be marked to indicate two-third and half of body capacity, max and min level.
6.8 Fully dimensioned sectional drawing submitted; basic minimum dimensions include flange/lug thickness for
body, thickness for lid, wall thickness of body at the middle, cooker base thickness.

7 WORKMANSHIP AND FINISH
7.1 Body and lid free from wrinkles, scratches, and other surface defects (naked eye or corrected vision).
7.2 Body and lid neat and clean.
7.3 Brass parts (except screws, studs, nuts) plated chromium over nickel; plating conform to service condition number 2
of IS 1068 (adhesion and thickness tested). Corrosion test per IS 1068 is a type test.
7.4 Cooker, if hard anodized, fully or partially, shall satisfy Annex E (anodized portions only).

8 TESTS
8.1 Air Pressure Test — subjected to air pressure of 40 percent of the nominal cooking steam pressure, slowly raised
to nominal cooking steam pressure; shall not show any sign of leakage or deformation.
8.2 Proof Pressure Test — as prescribed in Annex F; no sign of leakage or other forms of failure during or after.
8.3 Operating Test for Pressure Regulating Device.
8.3.1 The PRD shall operate within ± 20 percent of the nominal cooking steam pressure as declared by the manufacturer.
Pass test in Annex G. The drop in pressure after operation of PRD shall not be more than 40 percent of the average
measured cooking steam pressure (see G-2).
8.3.2 Each PRD tested before assembly per 8.3.1; not more than 5 percent rejection.
8.4 Test for Safety Relief Device.
8.4.1 Device shall satisfy the test in Annex H. After the test, the cooker shall be in normal serviceable condition.
8.4.2 Test 5 samples of destructible safety relief device per 8.4.1; result shall be within a range of ± 20 percent of the mean values.
8.5 Bursting Pressure Test — bursting pressure shall be not less than 6 kgf/cm2. The cooker shall pass test per Annex J.
Pressure developed approximate 10 kgf/cm2 in reservoir and released gradually. May deform but shall not show any incipient
fracture nor any crack. Separation of base/bottom of composite cooker (see 8.8) is acceptable.
8.6 Tests for Removal of Lid Under Pressure.
8.6.1 For Outer Lid Cooker — A force of 12 kgf (120 N approximately), applied to lid/cover or its fastening/locking device,
shall not be able to release the lid/cover unless the steam pressure inside has fallen below 0.18 kgf/cm2 (18 kN/m2) gauge (see Annex K).
8.6.2 For Inner Lid Cooker — A force of 12 kgf (120 N approximately) shall not be able to release the lid/cover unless the
steam pressure inside has fallen below 0.18 kgf/cm2 (18 kN/m2) gauge (see Annex L).
8.6.3 For Cooker Fastened by Screw Clamps or Any Other Locking Device — 8.6.3.1 A force of 12 kgf (120 N approximately)
applied to the opening device; lid/cover shall not open until the steam pressure has fallen below 0.18 kgf/cm2 (18 kN/m2) gauge (see Annex T).
8.6.4 Electrical pressure cooker — lid cannot be removed while pressure within container is excessive; per IS 302 (Part 2/Sec 15).
8.7 Test for Spring Loaded Mechanism — spring compressed and kept 24 h; then compressed and released 3 000 times consecutively;
after test, spring shall not suffer a permanent set of more than 3 percent in length.
8.8 Test for stainless steel cookers with composite base/bottom — refer tests in IS 15960.
8.9 Stainless steel, aluminium induction compatible base and 3-ply cooker tested per Annex M (on a separate cooker).
8.10 Handles tested per IS 13395: a) Resistance to burning; b) Heat resistance; c) Torque strength; d) Bending strength;
e) Fatigue resistance; f) Thermal insulation test. (Handle tests done at least once a year — type tests.)
8.11 External Coating — 8.11.1 powder/ceramic/high temperature resistant liquid coating per thickness test, adhesion test as per
IS 9730; coating comply IS 9806; minimum thickness declared by the manufacturer.
8.11.1.1 Dry heat the cooker body on gas stove to 220 +5/-0 °C and quench in water at ambient; repeat 25 times. No blistering of coating.
8.12 Internal Coating — non-stick coating shall conform to IS 9730.
8.13 Pressure cooker with integral electrical heating device shall conform to IS 302 (Part 2/Sec 15).
8.14 For 3-ply construction cooker body — tested per Annex R; no separation/delamination of 3 ply nor blisters after 50 cycles.
8.15 Test(s) on Tempered Glass Lid.
8.15.1 Fragmentation Test — sample lid broken by center punch; particle count completed within 3 min. There shall be not less than
60 particles in a square of side 50 mm (50 mm × 50 mm).
8.15.2 Ball Drop Test — smooth surface steel ball of 225 +0/-5 g placed at a minimum height of 1 000 mm; free fall on three
points; no crack shall be seen by the naked eye under normal light.
8.15.3 Thermal Shock Test — air circulating oven at (120 ± 5) °C, soak 30 min, remove and pour one litre of water at ambient;
lid shall not shatter. (Applicable for glass lids used for cooking utensils; optional for others.)
8.15.4 Free Fall Test — Glass lid with rim: drop from 900 mm onto vitrified tile/level concrete; glass shall not break/crack.
Glass lid without rim: drop from 900 mm; glass shall not break/crack.
8.16 If the PRD is provided with the chain (see 6.1.6), the chain shall be tested per 8.16.1.
8.16.1 Hang a weight of three times the weight of PRD (weight valve) for 4 h; no deformation or breakage of chain or its locating area.
8.17 Test for Effectiveness for Induction Bottom — applicable to induction bottom cooker (includes SS and 3-ply induction
compatible). This test is a type test. Tested per Annex S. After test, cooker shall be stable on gas stove or induction cooktop.
If the induction plate detaches during or after the test, the cooker fails the test.

ANNEX A — List of Referred Standards (IS 21, IS 228, IS 302, IS 319, IS 733, IS 739, IS 740, IS 1068, IS 1342, IS 4246,
IS 4454 (Part 4), IS 4905, IS 5522, IS 5523, IS 6012, IS 6057, IS 6527, IS 6528, IS 6603, IS 6911, IS 7466, IS 9730,
IS 9806, IS 13395, IS 15960, IS 15997).

ANNEX B (Clause 4.1) — Gross Capacity Test: filled with water up to brim of vent pipe; volume measured = gross capacity.
ANNEX C (Clause 6.3.1) — Test for Temperature Responsive Safety Relief Device: water 1/16 of internal volume; the temperature
responsive device shall release before a gauge pressure not greater than 3 kgf/cm2 (300 kN/m2 approximately) is reached.
ANNEX D (Clause 6.3.2) — Temperature Test for Cookers Without Fusible Safety Relief Device (type test).
ANNEX E (Clause 7.4) — Test for Hard Anodized Surface. E-2 Thickness: minimum thickness of anodized layer shall be 25 µm.
E-4 Hardness: E-4.1 Type Test — hardness by micro-indentation, tested once in 6 month or once in 2 lakh cooker produced
(whichever is greater); the hardness should be more than 350 HV. E-5 Resistance to staining (Annex N) — no staining visible.
E-6 Alkali resistance (Annex P) — no loss of insulating properties.
ANNEX F (Clauses 8.2, 5.2.1 and 5.2.2) — Proof Pressure Test: a pressure not less than twice the greatest nominal cooking
pressure shall be applied; cooker shall not show any sign of leakage or other forms of failure during or after the test.
ANNEX G (Clauses 8.3.1 and 8.4.1) — Operating Test for PRD: operating pressure shall be within ± 20 percent of the nominal
cooking pressure declared by the manufacturer; note five readings for operating pressure and pressure drops, take average.
ANNEX H (Clauses 8.4.1 and S-3) — Test for Safety Relief Device. Water 1/16 of internal volume.
H-1.1 For Destructible Devices — deemed to have passed if it functions when steam reaches a gauge pressure between 1.25 and 3
times the greatest nominal cooking pressure.
H-1.2 For Gasket Release System — deemed to have passed if it functions when steam reaches a gauge pressure between 1.25 and 2.5
times the greatest nominal cooking pressure.
H-1.3 For Resettable Devices (other than H-1.2) — H-1.3.1 deemed to have passed if it functions when steam reaches a gauge
pressure between 1.25 and 3 times the greatest nominal cooking pressure. H-1.3.2 tested 25 times as per H-1.3.1 (type test).
ANNEX J (Clause 8.5) — Bursting Pressure Test: gradually increasing hydraulic pressure until rupture or appreciable leakage;
maximum gauge pressure obtained taken as bursting pressure. If the cooker satisfactorily stands a pressure of 6 kgf/cm2, further
applications of pressure is not necessary.
ANNEX K (Clause 8.6.1) — Tests for Removal of Lid Under Pressure for Outer Lid Cooker: K-12 Put a load of 12 kgf; the cooker
should not open until the pressure inside has fallen below 0.18 kgf/cm2.
ANNEX L (Clause 8.6.2) — Tests for Removal of Lid Under Pressure for Inner Lid Cooker: L-12 Put a load of 12 kgf; the cooker
should not open until the pressure inside has fallen below 0.18 kgf/cm2.
ANNEX M (Clause 8.9) — Test for Stainless Steel, Aluminium Induction Compatible Base and 3-Ply Cooker: dry heat 250 +25/-0 °C
and quench, 25 cycles gas + 25 cycles induction (total 50). Induction steel base shall not peel off or separate; 3-ply shall not
show separation (delamination) nor blisters.
ANNEX N (Clause E-5) — Resistance to Staining of Anodized Coatings (nitric acid + dye, visual).
ANNEX P (Clause E-6) — Resistance to Alkali of Anodized Aluminium (5 percent NaOH, multimeter conductivity).
ANNEX Q (Clauses 5.2.1, 5.2.2, 5.5, 5.6, 5.7, 5.8 and 6.4.2.1) — Stainless Steel Property. Q-1.1 chemical composition (in percent):
C : 0.15 Max ; Si : 1.00 Max ; Mn : 2.00 Max ; S : 0.15 to 0.25 ; P : 0.20 Max ; Cr : 17.0 to 19.0 ; Ni : 8.0 to 10.0.
Q-1.2 analysis per IS 228.
ANNEX R (Clause 8.14) — Test for Adhesion of 3-Ply Construction Cooker Body: heat 250 +25/-0 °C and quench; repeat 50 cycles.
ANNEX S (Clause 8.17) — Test for Effectiveness for Induction Bottom: fill 1/16 internal volume; note timing for first PRD operation;
repeat; the time noted after this test shall not be more than 25 percent of the time noted after S-2. (Minimum 1 800 W induction cooktop.)
ANNEX T (Clause 8.6.3.1) — Tests for Removal of Lid Under Pressure for Cooker Fastened by Screw Clamps or Any Other Locking
Device: T-10 A force of 12 kgf (120 N approximately) applied to opening device; lid/cover shall not open until the steam pressure
inside has fallen below 0.18 kgf/cm2 (18 kN/m2) gauge.
ANNEX U (Clause 10.4) — Important Safeguards for Electric Pressure Cooker.
