FOREWORD
This Indian Standard (Part 1) (Second Revision) was adopted by BIS after the draft finalized by the Power Cables
Sectional Committee had been approved by the Electrotechnical Division Council. First published 1977, revised 1988;
this revision aligns with international practices. Published in three parts: Part 2 (3.3 kV up to and including 33 kV),
Part 3 (66 kV up to and including 220 kV). All amendments to first revision incorporated.
Attention: cables with Category C3 (see Annex A), sheathed with low smoke and halogen free (LSHF) compound, are
mechanically not comparable with other categories; not intended for outdoor installations; handle and install with
sufficient care against mechanical damage, corrosive substances or solvents, UV radiation and excessive heat.
Assistance derived from IEC 60502-1. Rounding off per IS 2 : 2022.

SECTION 1 GENERAL
1 SCOPE
1.1 This standard (Part 1) covers requirements for both armoured and unarmoured single, twin, three, four and
multi core cross-linked polyethylene (XLPE) insulated and thermoplastic sheathed cables for electric supply and
control purposes.
1.2 Suitable for ac single phase or three phase (earthed or unearthed) systems for rated voltages up to and
including 1 100 V; may be used on dc systems for rated voltages up to and including 1 500 V.
NOTE — Cables may be operated continuously at a power frequency voltage 10 percent higher than rated voltage.
1.3 Armoured cables are suitable for use in mines also; additional requirements at 4.2, 14.5.2 and 18.2.
1.4 Suitable where combination of ambient temperature and temperature rise due to load results in conductor
temperature not exceeding 90 °C under normal operation and 250 °C under short-circuit condition.
1.5 Also covers cables with improved fire performance, categories C1, C2 and C3 (Annex A); additional
requirements at 8.2, 8.4, 16.1, 16.2 and 18.2.1.
NOTE — Cables with PVC outer sheath can be classified as meeting requirement of Category 01.

2 REFERENCES — see Annex B.

3 TERMINOLOGY
3.1 Routine Test — carried out by manufacturer on all finished cable lengths to check requirements likely to vary
during manufacture. 3.2 Type Test — to prove quality and design of a given type of cable; need not be repeated
unless changes in cable materials or design; type approval valid for other conductor material/cross-section if same
grade of insulation (XLPE) and manufacturing process and conductor cross-sectional area not larger than tested.
3.3 Acceptance Tests — on samples from a lot for acceptance of the lot. 3.4 Optional Tests — by agreement.
3.5 Earthed System / 3.6 Unearthed System — definitions as printed (fault-condition voltage ≤ 80 percent of
nominal system voltage for earthed neutral, protective cut-out arrangement, or arc suppression coil with isolation
within 1 h (non-radial field cables) / 8 h (radial cables), total ≤ 125 h per year).

11 CORE IDENTIFICATION
11.1 a) coloured strip applied on the core (red/yellow/blue for phases, black for reduced neutral); b) colouring of
XLPE insulation: single core — red, black, yellow, blue or natural; two core — red and black; three core — red,

and above — two adjacent cores (counting and direction core) in each layer blue and yellow, remaining cores
grey; c) by numerals (up to 4 cores): two core 0,1; three core 0,1,2; four core 0,1,2,3; control cables: 0 counting

11.2 Reduced neutral core black. 11.3 More than 4 cores: alternatively numbered sequentially starting 1 in inner
layer, hindu-arabic numerals, same contrasting colour, legible. 11.4 Reduced neutral core numbered '0'.

14.5.2 Mining cables: armour resistance shall not exceed conductor resistance per IS 8130 by more than

SECTION 4 TESTS
16 CLASSIFICATION OF TESTS
16.1 Type Tests: i) conductor — annealing (Cu, Part 1), tensile (Al, Part 2), wrapping (Al, Part 3), resistance
(IS 8130, Part 5); ii) round/formed steel wire armour — dimensions (14.3, Part 36); physical: tensile strength
(14.6a, Part 37), elongation at break (14.6b, Part 37), torsion (round, 14.6c, Part 38), winding (formed, 14.6d,
Part 39), uniformity of zinc coating (14.6e, Part 40), mass of zinc coating (14.6f, Part 41), resistivity (14.6g,
Part 42); iii) thickness of insulation and sheath (10, 13, 15; Tables 3, 5 and 7; Part 6); iv) physical tests for
insulation — tensile strength and elongation at break (Table 1, Part 7), ageing in air oven (Part 11), hot set
(Part 30), shrinkage (Part 12), water absorption gravimetric (Part 33); v) physical tests for outer sheath — PVC:
tensile/elongation (IS 5831, Part 7), ageing (Part 11), loss of mass (Part 10), shrinkage (Part 12), hot deformation
(Part 15), heat shock (Part 14), thermal stability (Part 60); PE: carbon black (Table 1A, Part 32), tensile/elongation
before and after ageing (Part 7), hot deformation (Part 15), shrinkage (Part 12); LSHF: tensile/elongation before
and after ageing (Table 1B, Part 7), hot deformation (Part 15), water absorption (ST8, Part 33), shrinkage (ST12,
Part 12); vi) insulation resistance (volume resistivity, Table 1, Part 43); vii) high voltage test (17.2, Part 45);
viii) flammability test (17.3, Part 53); ix) additional tests for improved fire performance categories (Annex A):
C1 — oxygen index (17.4, Part 58), temperature index (17.9, Part 64), flame retardance single cable (17.5,
Part 61), flame retardance bunched cables (17.6, Part 62); C2 — C1 tests plus smoke density (17.8, IS 13360
(Part 6/Sec 9)) and halogen acid evolution (17.7, Part 59); C3 — C2 tests plus light transmission (17.11, Part 63)
and pH and conductivity (17.10).
16.2 Acceptance Tests: annealing (Cu); tensile (Al); wrapping (Al); conductor resistance; thickness of insulation
and sheath; hot set for insulation; tensile strength and elongation at break for insulation and sheath; high voltage
test; insulation resistance (volume resistivity); additional category tests — C1: oxygen index, flame retardance
single and bunched; C2: + halogen acid, smoke density; C3: + light transmission, pH and conductivity.
Recommended sampling plan per Annex D.
16.3 Routine Tests: conductor resistance (IS 8130, Part 5); high voltage test (17.2, Part 45); resistance test for
armour (mining cable only, 14.5.1, Part 42).
16.4 Optional Tests: cold bend test for outer sheath (OD > 12.5 mm) (IS 5831 or Table 1B, Part 20); cold impact
test for outer sheath (Part 21); cold elongation test for LSHF sheath (Table 1B, Part 7); resistance test for armour
(other than mining cable) (14.5.1, Part 42).

17 DETAIL OF TESTS
17.1 General — per appropriate parts of IS 10810 with additional information in this standard.
17.2 High Voltage Test at Room Temperature (type, acceptance, routine): withstand 3 kV ac (rms) 40-60 Hz or
dc 7.2 kV, between conductors and between conductors and ECC (if any), 5 min per test connection.
17.3 Flammability Test: period of burning after removal of flame ≤ 60 s; unaffected (uncharred) portion from
lower edge of top clamp ≥ 50 mm.
17.4 Oxygen Index Test (FR, FR-LSH or LSHF sheath): samples of inner/outer sheath at 27 °C ± 2 °C or room
temperature; oxygen index ≥ 29.
17.5 Flame Retardance Test on Single Cables (OD < 35 mm) (FR, FR-LSH or LSHF sheathed): no visible
damage on test specimen within 300 mm from its upper end (marks from mixing devices, soot, colour change
not considered damage).
17.6 Flame Retardance Test on Bunched Cables (FR, FR-LSH or LSHF sheathed): after burning ceases, charred/
affected portion ≤ 2.5 m above bottom edge of burner (front and rear). NOTE — Category B and C methods of
IS 10810 (Part 62) used; in absence of special requirements for method B, method C used for both C1 and C2.
17.7 Halogen Acid Gas Evolution (FR-LSH or LSHF outer sheath): HCl evolved ≤ 20 percent by weight
(FR-LSH); ≤ 0.5 percent (LSHF).
17.8 Smoke Density (FR-LSH or LSHF sheath): smoke density rating ≤ 60 (FR-LSH); ≤ 20 (LSHF).
17.9 Temperature Index (FR, FR-LSH or LSHF sheath): extrapolated temperature at which oxygen index is 21
shall be ≥ 250 °C.
17.10 pH and Conductivity of LSHF Sheath: per Annex F of IS 17505 (Part 1) [pH ≥ 4.3; conductivity
≤ 10 µS/mm per Table 1B].
17.11 Light Transmission for LSHF Sheathed Cables: per IS 10810 (Part 63); light transmittance ≥ 70 percent (Min).

ANNEX A — CLASSIFICATION OF CABLES FOR IMPROVED FIRE PERFORMANCE
01 — cables in open areas — flame retardant, single self-extinguishing, does not propagate fire.
C1 (FR) — constrained areas — flame retardant, single self-extinguishing, does not propagate fire even in groups
in vertical ducts.
C2 (FR-LSH) — constrained areas with limited human activities and/or sophisticated systems — flame retardant
with reduced halogen evolution and smoke.
C3 (LSHF) — constrained areas, tunnels, airways etc — flame retardant with zero halogen evolution and low
smoke, adequate light transmission.
